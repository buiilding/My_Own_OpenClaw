## low: Fixed temp filenames make concurrent config/auth saves race-prone

status: open

- frontend/src/main/ipc/ipc_install_auth_state.cjs:57-72 (saveInstallAuthStateToDisk)

Both persistence helpers write to a deterministic temp path ending in .tmp and then rename it into place.
