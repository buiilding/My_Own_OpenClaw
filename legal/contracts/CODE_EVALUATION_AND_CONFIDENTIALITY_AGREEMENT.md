## 1. Purpose

Owner may disclose certain source code, repositories, documentation, architecture, business plans, and technical know-how solely so Evaluator can evaluate a potential partnership, collaboration, or role with Owner (the "**Purpose**").

Evaluator may use Confidential Information **only** for the Purpose and for no other purpose.

---

## 2. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of Owner, whether in written, oral, visual, electronic, or other form, including without limitation:

1. Source code, object code, scripts, APIs, prompts, model configs, pipelines, datasets, architecture diagrams, product roadmaps, and technical documentation.
2. Repository contents, commits, branches, issues, pull requests, tickets, CI/CD configurations, infrastructure details, credentials locations, and deployment details.
3. Business, commercial, strategic, and financial information.
4. Any notes, analyses, summaries, derivations, reproductions, or extracts prepared by Evaluator that are based on the above.

Confidential Information does **not** include information Evaluator proves by written records:

1. Was publicly available at the time of disclosure (without breach of this Agreement),
2. Became public through no fault of Evaluator,
3. Was lawfully known to Evaluator before disclosure by Owner, or
4. Was independently developed by Evaluator without using Owner’s Confidential Information.

---

## 3. Non-Disclosure and Non-Use Obligations

Evaluator shall:

1. Keep Confidential Information strictly confidential and use at least reasonable care, and no less than the care Evaluator uses for its own most sensitive information.
2. Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except as expressly permitted by this Agreement.
3. Not copy, reproduce, distribute, publish, post, share, or transmit Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose.
4. Not use Confidential Information to build, train, improve, benchmark, replicate, or commercialize any competing or derivative product, service, or codebase.
5. Not reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract proprietary logic from provided materials beyond what is strictly necessary for the Purpose.
6. Immediately notify Owner in writing of any actual or suspected unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

---

## 4. Access Restrictions

Evaluator shall:

1. Access repositories/systems only through accounts approved by Owner.
2. Keep access credentials secure; no sharing of credentials.
3. Use only authorized devices and secure storage.
4. Not remove or bypass access controls, audit controls, branch protections, or security safeguards.
5. Limit access to persons who are explicitly approved in writing by Owner and who sign obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.
6. Acknowledge Owner may immediately suspend or revoke repository/system access upon any suspected breach of this Agreement, pending investigation.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property Ownership

1. **Owner retains all rights, title, and interest** in and to all Confidential Information and all related intellectual property.
2. No license, transfer, assignment, or ownership right is granted to Evaluator by disclosure, except the limited, revocable right to use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.
3. Any unauthorized use is a material breach.

1. Evaluator may provide suggestions, comments, or feedback ("**Feedback**").  
2. Evaluator assigns to Owner all right, title, and interest in Feedback related to Owner’s Confidential Information and Project.
3. Unless a separate written agreement is signed by both Parties, **no joint ownership, partnership equity, or compensation rights are created** by this Agreement.

## 7. No Circumvention / No Unauthorized Exploitation

During the Term and for **one (1) month** after termination, Evaluator shall not, directly or indirectly:

1. Use Owner’s Confidential Information to solicit Owner’s customers, investors, suppliers, or strategic counterparties for a competing effort.
2. Use Owner’s Confidential Information to create a substitute offering intended to displace Owner.

This section does not restrict lawful competition based solely on information that is not Confidential Information.

---

## 8. Legal Compulsion

If Evaluator is legally compelled (e.g., subpoena, court order) to disclose Confidential Information, Evaluator shall, to the extent legally permitted:

1. Promptly notify Owner in writing before disclosure,
2. Cooperate with Owner in seeking protective treatment, and
3. Disclose only the minimum portion legally required.

1. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for **one (1) month** unless earlier terminated in writing by either Party (the "**Term**").
2. Confidentiality and non-use obligations survive termination for **five (5) years**, and for trade secrets, for so long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

Evaluator may retain one archival copy only if required by law, subject to continuing confidentiality obligations.
